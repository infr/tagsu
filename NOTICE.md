## Rakettitiede Oy

Tagsu is a badge made by [Rakettitiede Oy](https://rakettitiede.com/). This editor is unofficial and not affiliated with them. The following data comes from the badge and is not covered by the MIT license; it remains Rakettitiede's:

- `src/tagsu/lib/sprites.ts`: the animation sprites, as stored in the badge's firmware
- `BUILTIN` in `src/tagsu/lib/logos.ts`: the ten built-in logos, as published in the original editor

No firmware is included.

## Hershey fonts
